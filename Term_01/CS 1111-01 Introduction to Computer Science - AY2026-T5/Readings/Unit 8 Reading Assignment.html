Unit 8 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. In what ways do supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning differ, and how might they complement each other in various applications?
  2. Why is cloud computing considered a fundamental element in the digital age, and how does it impact our daily use of the internet?
  3. What are the practical implications of leveraging big data for decision-making, and how does blockchain address the challenges of traditional databases?
  4. How can IoT and robotics enhance efficiency and convenience in everyday life?
  5. What are the diverse applications of virtual reality, and how might they impact different industries?
