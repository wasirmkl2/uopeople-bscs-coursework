Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. Why have you decided to begin college now?
  2. Where are resources in the UoPeople Virtual Campus?
  3. How does poor netiquette make you feel?
  4. Why is good netiquette required in our learning community?

Read

  1. Baldwin, A., August, L., Bennett, J., Mathues, S., Monroe, S., O'Leary, MJ., Pearson, A., Troesh, J., & Watts, M. (2020). College success. OpenStax. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://openstax.org/details/books/college-success
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 1: College Success
        • Why are you in college? This chapter supports your reflection to find your reason for enrolling, discusses adjustment and vocabulary used. 
  2. The Institute for Learning and Teaching. (2022). Netiquette. Colorado State University. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://tilt.colostate.edu/Netiquette-Ground-Rules-For-Online-Discussions/
        • This article explains the basic netiquette to engage in online discussions. In a formal classroom discussion, students engage in structured conversations, respecting diverse viewpoints while articulating their perspectives effectively. Clarity, professionalism, and adherence to academic standards will support meaningful and productive exchanges of ideas. The ground rules highlight specific techniques while explaining why they are important.
  3. University of the People. (n.d.). Netiquette: Do’s and don’ts of general netiquette. https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/6863/topics/24413
        • This article includes a comprehensive overview defining netiquette and listing practices to use or avoid. A link to The General Code of Conduct in the University Catalog is included to learn the policy.
  4. University of the People. (n.d.). Time management . https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/6863/topics/24416
        • Time is a precious resource, it cannot be recaptured or replenished once it has passed. While reading, consider how you will organize your time. Online classes differ from traditional classrooms. Review the tips for online students and strategies to remain motivated.

    Watch

    1. Cambrian College Teaching and Learning Innovation Hub. (2019, September 3). Netiquette guidelines: For online forums and debates [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video showcases applications of netiquette in the course. Written communication and formal classroom discussions are essential components of academic success. Maintaining a positive and respectful tone promotes the growth of the learning community. Similar to face-to-face interactions, different etiquette applies when chatting with friends compared to formal settings. How you engage with classmates, professors, and university personnel contributes to developing our learning community.
