Unit 3 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. Why are references used for academic communication?
  2. What recommendations can be applied when reviewing resources?
  3. Why is it important to cite sources for academic writing?
  4. What are the most common types of academic dishonesty?
  5. What is the structure of a five-paragraph essay?

Read

  1. Baldwin, A., August, L., Bennett, J., Mathues, S., Monroe, S., O'Leary, MJ., Pearson, A., Troesh, J., & Watts, M. (2020). College success. OpenStax. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://openstax.org/details/books/college-success
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 8: Communicating
        • Section 8.1-8.5
          Communication serves various purposes, utilizes different resources, and takes multiple forms. This chapter defines communication, explores its purposes, and encourages comparison with personal experiences. Enhancing communication will reduce barriers leading to improved writing and critical thinking. 
  2. Georgetown University Library. (2025, January 27). Artificial Intelligence (generative) resources. https://guides.library.georgetown.edu/ai/ethics
        • The resource highlighted the dangers of using AI explored in Week 2. This week’s focus shifts to the Related Recommendations section, which presents strategies to mitigate these risks.
  3. University of the People. (n.d.).  Essay writing - an introduction. https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/6863/topics/24317
        • The Learning Resource Center (LRC) Academic Writing section should be used to develop the outline of the five-paragraph essay. Start with the Essay Writing - Overview and advance to the next topics in the section.
  4. University of the People. (n.d.). Databases for UoPeople students. https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/7172/topics/51910
        • UoPeople provides students with free access to two academic databases, LIRN and JSTOR. Read the Tips for Searching to learn how to use them. There is a video in each to guide you step-by-step.
          • Tips for Searching LIRN
          • Tips for Searching Using JSTOR
  5. Mamak, L., & Mosher, L. (2024). APA citations for content generated by Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools. Normandale Community College Library. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://www.normandale.edu/_files/documents/library/ai-apa.pdf
        • Artificial Intelligence is trained to always respond, the result can be misinformation and bias.  Facts must be researched to confirm accuracy and when using the content, give credit to the source.  This document includes how to give credit to AI generated content and reinforces the requirement of transparency.
