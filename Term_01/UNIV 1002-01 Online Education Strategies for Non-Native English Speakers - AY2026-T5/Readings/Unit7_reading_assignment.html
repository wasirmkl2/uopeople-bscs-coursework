Unit 7 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. Is time management different as a university student than you were used to?
  2. What behaviors can help or hinder when it comes to managing time?
  3. What are the best ways to make sure tasks are completed?
  4. What are S.M.A.R.T. goals?
  5. How will you use time management strategies to enhance your success as a learner?

Read

    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 3: Managing Your Time and Priorities
