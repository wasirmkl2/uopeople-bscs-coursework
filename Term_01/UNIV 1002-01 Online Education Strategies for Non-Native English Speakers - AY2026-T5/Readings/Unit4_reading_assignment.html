Unit 4 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What are the pros and cons of online reading?
  2. How can distinguishing between reading types help you academically and personally?
  3. Why is academic reading time investment greater than other reading applications?
  4. How will reading and note taking skills help you study effectively?
  5. How are resources evaluated for accuracy?
  6. Is there a relationship between information literacy to personal beliefs?

Read

  1. Baldwin, A., August, L., Bennett, J., Mathues, S., Monroe, S., O'Leary, MJ., Pearson, A., Troesh, J., & Watts, M. (2020). College success. OpenStax. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://openstax.org/details/books/college-success
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 5: Reading and Note-Taking
        • There are different purposes for reading and note-taking that will be used while you are in college. The benefits and strategies of active reading are explored, along with activities to assess your time requirements. You will need to be able to use the notes to study for exams and recall strategies to adopt. The chapter examines the reasons, presents different techniques, and demonstrates examples.  
  2. Northern Kentucky University. (2022). 4.2 Evaluating with SIFT. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://saalck.pressbooks.pub/infolit/chapter/evaluating-sift/
        • SIFT is an acronym that describes the steps to evaluate resources. Stop, Investigate, Find Better Coverage and Trace the sources are the steps. Consider how these steps can be included in your everyday research.
  3. University of the People. (n.d.). Evaluating sources. https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/6863/topics/24336
        • This is a source evaluation checklist for the CRAAP model. CRAAP is the acronym for Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy and Purpose. CRAAP was outlined in the early 2000s and examines the content of a document. SIFT enhances this by investigating the online source and looking for other coverage to determine its usefulness.
  4. University of the People. (n.d.). How to check digital sources of information. https://learn.uopeople.edu/d2l/le/lessons/6863/topics/24335
        • The resource on Evaluating a Website from the Excelsior Online Reading Lab showcases eight different purposes of website and ten questions to guide identification of the value.
