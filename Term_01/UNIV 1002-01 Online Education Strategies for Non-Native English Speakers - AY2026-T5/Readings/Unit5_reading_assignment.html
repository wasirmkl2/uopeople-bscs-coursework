Unit 5 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What values shape your decisions when engaging with the community?
  2. Are your academic goals and career goals aligned?
  3. What are the four C’s to apply when developing feedback?
  4. How will developing self-assessment skills improve feedback to peers?
