Unit 8 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What is mental health?
  2. What is the importance of mental and physical self-care?
  3. What strategies can be used to manage stress?
  4. Is test anxiety real?
  5. What can you do on the exam day to increase confidence and success?

Read

  1. 1. Baldwin, A., August, L., Bennett, J., Mathues, S., Monroe, S., O'Leary, MJ., Pearson, A., Troesh, J., &amp; Watts, M. (2020). College success. OpenStax. Licensed under CC 4.0. https://openstax.org/details/books/college-success
    • View the Online Book
    • Read Chapter 11: Engaging in a Healthy Lifestyle
      • Stress, whether positive or negative, is a natural part of life. This chapter explores its impact on both physical and mental health. There is no one-size-fits-all approach to self-care, as each person’s needs are unique. However, the importance of self-care is recognized, and various strategies are presented to help you better manage stress and prioritize your well-being.
    • Read Chapter 6 Section 6.3 - Test Taking
      • Test anxiety is real and can lead to self-doubt, causing you to underperform or overcompensate to the point where your exam results suffer. However, don’t be discouraged—you can still succeed even if you experience test anxiety. This chapter will help you better understand test anxiety while focusing on preparation, managing expectations, creating an optimal test environment, and strategies for after the exam.
