Unit 6 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the video, consider the following:

  1. What is your role in the learning process?
  2. What are your learning strengths and areas to develop?
  3. What are the differences between a fixed and growth mindset?
  4. Why does negative bias defeat you compared to the power of yet?
  5. How will you become resilient and grittier?
  6. How can you take advantage of learning in a way that works for you?
