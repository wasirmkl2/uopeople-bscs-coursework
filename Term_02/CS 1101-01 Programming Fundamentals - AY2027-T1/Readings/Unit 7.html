Unit 7 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. How can businesses use the statement of cash flows to evaluate their ability to meet long-term financial obligations?
  2. What are the key differences between loans and bonds, and how do these differences affect financing decisions?
  3. Why is it important to distinguish between current and long-term liabilities when preparing financial statements?

Read

  1. Franklin, M. Graybeal, P. Cooper, D. Bean, L., Burt, I., Carr, S., Collins, D. T., Coram, S., Creech, K., Czyzewski, A., Gauci, M., Greenman, C., Haselkorn, M., Irujo, C., Johnson, C., Khanlarian, C., Lukshaitis, T., Luna, D., Nantz, B., … Woolley, D. (2022). Principles of accounting, volume 1: Financial accounting. Open Stax. Licensed under CC 4.0.
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 13: Long-Term Liabilities
        • Section 13.1 - Explaining the Pricing of Long-Term Liabilities
          • This section discusses the pricing of long-term funding and fundamentals of bonds. It focuses on how businesses use debt and equity-based financing to secure funds for operations or expansion.
        • Section 13.3 - Prepare Journal Entries to Reflect the Life Cycle of Bonds
          • This section explains journal entries related to bonds. It covers bond issuance, periodic interest payments, and repayment at maturity.
      • Read Chapter 16: Statement of Cash Flows
        • Section 16.2 - Differentiate between Operating, Investing, and Financing Activities 
          •  This section explains the statement of cash flows, which breaks down cash movements into operating, investing, and financing activities.
        • Section 16.3 - Prepare the Statement of Cash Flows Using the Indirect Method
          • This section outlines the preparation of the statement of cash flows using the indirect method, which adjusts net income for noncash activities and changes in working capital.  
        • Section 16.5 - Use Information from the Statement of Cash Flows to Prepare Ratios to Assess Liquidity and Solvency
          • This section explains the preparation of ratios that assess liquidity and solvency, utilizing the information derived from the cash flow statement. 
        • Section 16.6 - Appendix: Prepare a Completed Statement of Cash Flows Using the Direct Method 
          • This section explains how to prepare the cash flow statement using the direct method.
