Unit 4 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What is a problem statement?
  2. How can one write a good problem statement?
  3. What are SMART goals?
  4. How are goals different from objectives?
  5. How are research questions developed from objectives?

Read

  1. Serdikoff, S. (2021). Research Methods for the Behavioral Sciences [PDF]. (5th Edition). GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/psychology-textbooks/12/

    • Read Chapter 3: Overview of the Scientific Process
      • Section 13 – Generating Good Research Questions

  2. National University. (2024, October 30). Chapter 1: Problem Statement. Dissertation Center. Retrieved December 11, 2024, from https://resources.nu.edu/c.php?g=1006886&p=7294692

    • This chapter provides a good overview of the problem statement, with sample examples.

  3. Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy - Colorado College. (n.d.). https://www.coloradocollege.edu/other/assessment/how-to-assess-learning/learning-outcomes/blooms-revised-taxonomy.html

    • This webpage lists the useful verbs that can be used by the researchers to write their goals.

  4. CFI Team [CFI Team]. (2023, November 19). SMART goals. Corporate Finance Institute. Retrieved December 2, 2024, from https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/management/smart-goal/

    • This article explains the concept of SMART Goals, and how to ensure every step is met.

  5. Ryan, E. (2022, July 12). Research Objectives | Definition & Examples. Scribbr. Retrieved December 11, 2024, from https://www.scribbr.com/research-process/research-objectives/

    • This article explains and elaborates the concept of research objectives by giving clear examples.
