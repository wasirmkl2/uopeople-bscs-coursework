Unit 6 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are research methods?
  2. How does one determine the suitable method?
  3. What are various methods of communication in a formal setting?
  4. How can communication be made more effective?
  5. What are the benefits of effective communications?

Read

  1. Serdikoff, S. (2021). Research Methods for the Behavioral Sciences [PDF]. (5th Edition). GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/psychology-textbooks/12/
    • Read Chapter 3: Overview of the Scientific Process
      • Section 16 – Analyzing the Data
      • Section 17 – Drawing Conclusions and Reporting the Results
      • These sections provide an overview of the technicalities associated with analyzing data and reporting the results.
  2. College of Business and Economics. (2024, November 1). Guidelines for reports. https://www.boisestate.edu/cobe/cobe-writing-style-guide/guidelines-for-reports/ 
    • This web article provides an overview with clear examples on how to write formal reports by analyzing data.
