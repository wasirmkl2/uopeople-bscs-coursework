Unit 3 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are essential elements of analyzing literature for review?
  2. What are gaps in existing field?
  3. How can one look for gaps in existing literature?

Read

  1. Weaver, R., Bost, L., Kassorla, M., McKinney-Holley, K., Crowther, K., Curtright, L., Gilbert, N., Hall, B., Ravita, T., & Swenson, K. (2016). Successful College Composition (3rd Edition). GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/english-textbooks/8/
      • Read Chapter 4: Writing a Research Paper
        • Section 4.5: Evaluating and Processing Your Sources 
        • Section 4.6: Applying Your Research
  2. Ashman, M., Cruthers, A., eCampusOntario, Ontario Business Faculty, & University of Minnesota. (2021). 10.9: Evaluating sources [Online]. In Advanced Professional Communications. Pressbooks. https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/llsadvcomm/chapter/10-9-evaluating-sources/
    • This chapter from the online textbook gives a comprehensive understanding of how to evaluate reliable and credible sources of information.
  3. LibGuides: Conducting a Literature review: Analyzing the literature. (n.d.). https://libguides.libraries.wsu.edu/litreview/analysis
    • This webpage explains how to analyze sources and writing them.

  4. Essays, O. (2024, April 18). How to identify research gaps | Oxbridge Essays. Oxbridge Essays. https://www.oxbridgeessays.com/blog/how-to-identify-research-gaps/

    • This article explains various types of research gaps and how to identify them.

  5. Gap statements – write like a scientist. (n.d.). https://sites.middlebury.edu/middsciwriting/overview/organization/gap-statements/

    • This article explains how to effectively write the research gap in your paper after reviewing sources.

    Additional Resources

    1. LibGuides: Research Process: Literature Gap and Future Research. (n.d.). https://resources.nu.edu/researchprocess/literaturegap
