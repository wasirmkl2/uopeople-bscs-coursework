## 5. Key Policy Facts Referenced in This Plan

- UoPeople BS-CS = 120 credits = 40 courses (3 credits each).
- Standard transfer cap: 50% (20 courses / 60 credits); case-by-case exception up to 75%
  (30 courses / 90 credits) — not guaranteed.
- First 20 transferred courses from a Learning Partner (Sophia, StraighterLine, etc.):
  $17/course evaluation fee **waived**.
- Transfer credit carries no letter grade and does not affect UoPeople CGPA.
- Full-time students with CGPA ≥3.00 may register for up to 4 courses/term.
- 11 required proctored courses for BS-CS: MATH 1201, MATH 1280, CS 2205, CS 2301,
  CS 3303, CS 3305, CS 3306, CS 3307, CS 4402, CS 4407, HIST 1421.

*Sources: UoPeople Academic Catalog (catalog.uopeople.edu), UoPeople official Sophia
Recommended Course List (PDF), UoPeople Transfer Credit Policy PDFs.*
